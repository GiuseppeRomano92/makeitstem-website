1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through:

  • Contact Forms: Name, email address, career stage, message content, and which language version of the site you used

  • Group Session Recordings: Video and audio of group tutoring sessions, which may include your name, image, voice and anything you say or share in the session. You can keep your camera off if you prefer.
  • Email Communications: Any information you include in direct emails
  • Website Analytics: General usage data (pages visited, time spent, device type)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for:

  • Responding to your enquiries and contact requests
  • Scheduling and conducting mentoring and tutoring sessions
  • Recording group tutoring sessions and sharing the recordings with that group’s participants
  • Issuing certificates of attendance
  • Sending you updates about our services (with your consent)
  • Improving our website and services
  • Meeting legal and compliance obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: When you submit a contact form or sign up for mentoring
  • Contract: When we're providing mentoring services to you
  • Legitimate Interests: Improving our services and website security
GDPR Compliance: We fully comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored and processed in compliance with GDPR. We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your information. Data is stored only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • Withdraw Consent: Stop processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@makeitstem.com

7. Cookies & Analytics

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. We keep our use of them to a minimum, and we group them into two categories.

Essential — always active

These are needed for the site to work and do not require your consent. They include the record of your own cookie choice (stored in your browser as mis-cookie-consent, kept until you clear it).

Analytics — only with your consent

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are useful so we can improve the site. These cookies are blocked until you accept them. We go further than a standard cookie banner: the Google Analytics script is not loaded at all until you choose “Accept analytics”. If you reject, or simply ignore the banner, no request is ever made to Google Analytics and no analytics cookie is created. We also implement Google Consent Mode v2, with analytics storage set to “denied” by default.

CookiePurposeRetention

IP anonymisation is applied by default in Google Analytics 4, and we have not enabled any advertising, remarketing or ad-personalisation features.

Changing your mind. You can accept or reject analytics cookies at any time via Cookie Settings — also linked in the footer of every page. Rejecting them does not affect your access to any part of this site. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Legal basis. We rely on your consent for analytics cookies (under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 and UK GDPR), and on our legitimate interests for essential cookies that keep the site secure and functional.
